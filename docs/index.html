About Surinaamse Wetten

An open, fast, client-side digital reader and full-text search engine for the statutory laws, civil codes, labor acts, and regulations of Suriname.

Key Capabilities

  • Complete Corpus: Full statutory hierarchy organized by Books, Chapters, Titles, and Articles.
  • Sub-Millisecond Search: Search queries execute 100% locally in your browser using an in-memory inverted index.
  • Deep Linking: Every article has a direct shareable URL anchor.
  • Export & Citation: Built-in one-click tools to copy legal citations and clean text.
